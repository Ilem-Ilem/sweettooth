# HR System Implementation Roadmap

## Overview
The HR system currently has basic employee management, leave management, and probation review functionality. However, it lacks a comprehensive employee appraisal and performance management system. This document outlines the missing HR features that need to be implemented.

## Current HR Features (Implemented)
- ✅ Employee CRUD operations
- ✅ Role and permission management
- ✅ Leave management (applications, approvals, balances)
- ✅ Clock-in/out system
- ✅ Basic probation reviews
- ✅ Salary history tracking
- ✅ Employee step-outs
- ✅ Basic performance rating fields

## Missing HR Features (To Be Implemented)

### 1. Employee Appraisal System
### 2. Performance Management
### 3. Training & Development
### 4. Recruitment Management
### 5. Employee Relations
### 6. HR Analytics & Reporting
### 7. Compensation Management
### 8. Attendance & Time Tracking Enhancements
### 9. Employee Self-Service Portal
### 10. HR Compliance & Documentation

## Implementation Priority
1. **HIGH**: Employee Appraisal System (Core HR function)
2. **HIGH**: Performance Management (Linked to appraisals)
3. **MEDIUM**: Training & Development
4. **MEDIUM**: HR Analytics & Reporting
5. **LOW**: Recruitment Management
6. **LOW**: Employee Relations
7. **LOW**: Compensation Management enhancements
8. **LOW**: Employee Self-Service Portal
9. **LOW**: HR Compliance enhancements

## Technical Requirements
- Database migrations for new tables
- Eloquent models with relationships
- Livewire components for UI
- Service classes for business logic
- Approval workflows for HR actions
- Audit logging for HR changes
- Email notifications for HR events</content>
