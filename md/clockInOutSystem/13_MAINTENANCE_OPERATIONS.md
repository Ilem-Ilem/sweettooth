## Employee Handbook Updates

### Clock In/Out Policy Section

#### New Clock In/Out Policy

**Effective Date**: [Implementation Date]

**Purpose**: This policy outlines the procedures and expectations for clocking in and out of work shifts using the SweetTooth management system.

#### Clock In Requirements

1. **Timing Restrictions**
   - **Morning Shift**: Must clock in between 6:00 AM - 12:00 PM
   - **Afternoon Shift**: Must clock in between 12:00 PM - 8:00 PM
   - **Full Time**: No time restrictions apply

2. **Clock In Procedure**
   - Log into the SweetTooth system
   - Navigate to the shift selection page (automatic redirect for new sessions)
   - Select your assigned shift type
   - Click "Clock In" during the allowed time window
   - Wait for confirmation message

3. **Late Clock In Policy**
   - Clock in attempts outside the allowed time window will be rejected
   - Contact your supervisor immediately if you cannot clock in on time
   - Late arrivals may require approval and could affect attendance records

#### Clock Out Requirements

1. **Clock Out Procedure**
   - Use the "Clock Out" button in the dashboard header
   - Confirm the clock out action when prompted
   - For sales employees, complete shift closing if required
   - Wait for confirmation message

2. **Auto Clock Out**
   - System automatically clocks out employees after shift end time + grace period
   - Grace period: 15 minutes (configurable by management)
   - Auto clock out preserves worked time but may trigger additional review

#### Shift Management

1. **Active Shift Requirements**
   - All work functions require an active shift
   - System prevents access to work areas without active shift
   - Super administrators are exempt from this requirement

2. **Multiple Shifts**
   - Only one active shift allowed per day
   - Attempting to start a second shift will be rejected
   - Contact HR for special scheduling arrangements

#### Notifications and Alerts

1. **System Notifications**
   - Shift ending warnings (30 minutes, 10 minutes before end)
   - Auto clock out warnings (5 minutes before automatic clock out)
   - Overtime approaching alerts
   - Time violation notifications

2. **Email Notifications**
   - Auto clock out confirmations
   - Shift policy reminders
   - Important system updates

#### Troubleshooting

1. **Common Issues**
   - **"Too early for shift"**: Wait until shift start time
   - **"Too late for shift"**: Contact supervisor for approval
   - **"Already have active shift"**: Clock out of current shift first
   - **System errors**: Refresh page or contact IT support

2. **Getting Help**
   - Check the built-in Helper pages in each section
   - Contact your supervisor for policy questions
   - Contact IT support for technical issues
   - Use the employee portal for self-service options

#### Responsibilities

1. **Employee Responsibilities**
   - Clock in/out accurately and on time
   - Report system issues promptly
   - Follow shift timing policies
   - Keep login credentials secure

2. **Supervisor Responsibilities**
   - Monitor team attendance
   - Approve exceptions when appropriate
   - Address time violation patterns
   - Communicate policy changes

3. **HR Responsibilities**
   - Maintain accurate shift configurations
   - Process attendance-related requests
   - Monitor compliance with labor regulations
   - Provide training and support

## FAQ Documentation

### General Questions

**Q: Why do I have to clock in during specific time windows?**
A: This ensures accurate time tracking and helps maintain fair attendance policies across all employees.

**Q: What happens if I forget to clock out?**
A: The system automatically clocks you out after your shift end time plus a 15-minute grace period. You'll receive notifications as reminders.

**Q: Can I work from a different location?**
A: Yes, as long as you have internet access. The system works on any device with a web browser.

**Q: What if I need to leave early or arrive late?**
A: Contact your supervisor immediately. They can grant exceptions for legitimate reasons.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What if the system is slow or not responding?**
A: Refresh your browser first. If issues persist, contact IT support immediately.

**Q: Can I use my phone to clock in/out?**
A: Yes, the system is fully responsive and works on mobile devices.

**Q: What if I lose internet connection?**
A: Clock in/out as soon as connection is restored. Contact your supervisor to document any connectivity issues.

### Policy Questions

**Q: What counts as a "valid reason" for late clock in?**
A: Emergency situations, traffic accidents, medical issues, or other unforeseen circumstances. Always contact your supervisor immediately.

**Q: How does auto clock out affect my pay?**
A: Auto clock out preserves your actual worked time. However, repeated forgotten clock outs may require discussion with your supervisor.

**Q: Can I change my shift after clocking in?**
A: No, shift changes must be approved by your supervisor before clocking in. Contact them directly for scheduling changes.
