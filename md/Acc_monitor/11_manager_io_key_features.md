### Key Features of Manager.io (from Guides and Official Documentation)

Manager.io is a comprehensive, free (desktop edition) accounting software with extensive features for small to medium businesses. Below is a compiled list of major features inferred and extracted from the guides page and related documentation:

**Core Accounting Features:**
- General ledger and journal entries
- Chart of Accounts (fully customizable)
- Bank and cash accounts management
- Receipts and payments processing
- Inter-account transfers
- Bank reconciliations
- Expense claims
- Starting balances setup
- Recurring transactions
- Multi-currency support
- Tax codes and withholding tax management
- Reports generation (financial reports, cash flow statements, forecasts)

**Sales and Receivables:**
- Customer management
- Sales quotes, orders, invoices
- Credit notes
- Delivery notes
- Late payment fees
- Billable time and expenses
- Withholding tax receipts

**Purchases and Payables:**
- Supplier management
- Purchase quotes, orders, invoices
- Debit notes
- Goods receipts

**Inventory Management:**
- Inventory items tracking (with codes, names, unit costs)
- Inventory locations
- Inventory transfers (between locations)
- Inventory write-offs
- Inventory kits (bundling items for sale without physical assembly)
- Non-inventory items
- Integration with sales/purchases (automatic COGS calculation on sales)
- Production orders (basic manufacturing/assembly)

**Payroll and HR:**
- Employee management
- Payslips generation
- Payslip items customization

**Assets and Investments:**
- Fixed assets management with depreciation
- Intangible assets with amortization
- Investment tracking (including market prices)

**Customization and Advanced:**
- Custom fields
- Themes and document customization (footers, etc.)
- Email settings
- Divisions and projects
- Multiple businesses management
- Folders organization
- Bank rules (automation)
- Access tokens and user permissions (in cloud/server editions)
- Control accounts and special accounts
- Date/number formats and business localization

**Other:**
- Offline/desktop use (free), cloud/server editions available
- Cross-platform (Windows, Mac, Linux)
- No limits on data/transactions in free edition

The software emphasizes flexibility, with many features enabled optionally via settings.
