OVERVIEW
================================================================================

The accounting system includes comprehensive Asset and Liability management:

ASSETS (Current & Fixed)
├─ Current Assets: Cash, Banks, Receivables, Inventory
├─ Fixed Assets: Equipment, Building
└─ Accumulated Depreciation tracking

LIABILITIES (Current & Long-term)
├─ Current Liabilities: Payables, Taxes, Withholdings
├─ Long-term Liabilities: Loans
└─ Accrued Expenses

CURRENT ASSETS (GL Accounts 1000-1100)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. CASH ACCOUNTS
   GL Accounts:
   ├─ 1010: Cash - Head Office
   ├─ 1020: Cash - Branch A
   ├─ 1030: Cash - Branch B
   └─ 1040: Petty Cash
   
   Tracking:
   ├─ Models: CashPosition, DailyBankTransaction
   ├─ Daily reconciliation via CashPosition model
   ├─ Physical count vs book value tracking
   ├─ Variance investigation required
   
   GL Entries:
   ├─ Debit (increase): Cash receipts, Transfers in, Loan proceeds
   ├─ Credit (decrease): Payments made, Transfers out, ATM withdrawals
   
   Reconciliation:
   ├─ Daily: Count cash at end of day
   ├─ Compare to GL balance
   ├─ Record discrepancies with explanations
   ├─ Investigate overages (excess) or shortages (deficits)

2. BANK ACCOUNTS
   GL Accounts:
   ├─ 1050: Bank Account - Main
   ├─ 1060: Bank Account - Branch A
   └─ 1070: Bank Account - Branch B
   
   Tracking:
   ├─ Models: BankAccount, DailyBankPosition, DailyBankTransaction
   ├─ Daily bank positions recorded
   ├─ Pending/unreflected items tracked
   ├─ Bank reconciliation support
   
   Daily Position Tracking:
   ├─ Opening Balance (from previous day)
   ├─ Inflows (deposits, transfers in)
   ├─ Outflows (withdrawals, transfers out, charges)
   ├─ Available Balance
   
   Unreflected Items (DBP Excel Structure):
   ├─ Transfers B/F: In-transit transfers from previous days
   ├─ POS B/F: Pending POS deposits
   ├─ Transfers C/D: Transfers moving to next period
   ├─ POS C/D: POS deposits moving to next period
   
   Bank Reconciliation:
   ├─ Compare GL balance with actual bank statement
   ├─ Identify differences (timing, errors)
   ├─ Record timing differences as unreflected
   ├─ Investigate actual discrepancies

3. ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE (AR)
   GL Account: 1100 - Accounts Receivable
   
   Purpose:
   ├─ Track outstanding customer invoices (B2B sales)
   ├─ Monitor customer credit limits
   ├─ Track overdue payments
   
   GL Entries:
   ├─ Debit: Sale on credit (not yet paid)
   ├─ Credit: Payment received from customer
   ├─ Debit: Doubtful debt provision
   
   Future Implementation:
   ├─ Currently: Retail POS (cash/instant payment)
   ├─ When needed: Add B2B invoice module
   ├─ Aging reports (30, 60, 90+ days)
   ├─ Bad debt allowance


INVENTORY ASSETS (GL Accounts 1200-1220)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

GL Accounts:
├─ 1200: Inventory - Raw Materials
├─ 1210: Inventory - Work in Progress (WIP)
└─ 1220: Inventory - Finished Goods

Current System:
├─ Perpetual Inventory (real-time updates)
├─ Weighted Average Cost method
├─ Tracking in Stock model

GL Integration:
├─ COGS Entry (when sale): Debit COGS, Credit Inventory
├─ Purchase Entry: Debit Inventory, Credit Payables
├─ Adjustment Entry: Debit Loss, Credit Inventory (damage/shrinkage)
├─ Production: Debit WIP, Credit Raw Materials

Valuation:
├─ Inventory Value = Quantity × Average Cost
├─ Lower of cost or market (LCM) rule applied if needed
├─ Physical count vs perpetual records reconciled regularly

Reporting:
├─ Inventory balance sheet presentation
├─ COGS calculation (Sales - Inventory Change)
├─ Inventory turnover metrics
├─ Stock valuation reports


FIXED ASSETS (GL Accounts 1300-1400)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

GL Accounts:
├─ 1300: Fixed Assets - Equipment
├─ 1310: Fixed Assets - Building
└─ 1400: Accumulated Depreciation (contra-asset)

Asset Tracking:
├─ Asset Register (needs to be created)
   - Asset description, cost, date acquired
   - Serial number, location
   - Depreciation method, useful life, residual value
   - Regular maintenance/repair costs

Depreciation:
├─ Method: Straight-line (or choose MACRS/declining balance)
├─ Monthly Entry: Debit Depreciation Expense, Credit Accumulated Depreciation
├─ GL Account 7060: Depreciation Expense

Example:
├─ Freezer Equipment: ₦100,000
├─ Useful Life: 10 years
├─ Monthly Depreciation: ₦833.33
├─ GL Entry: Debit 7060 (₦833.33), Credit 1400 (₦833.33)

Balance Sheet Presentation:
├─ Fixed Assets (at cost): ₦1,500,000
├─ Less: Accumulated Depreciation: (₦250,000)
├─ Net Book Value: ₦1,250,000

PREPAID EXPENSES (GL Account 1500)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose:
├─ Track prepaid costs (insurance, rent, subscriptions)
├─ Allocate to expenses as benefits are consumed

GL Entries:
├─ Debit: When payment made
├─ Credit: Monthly as used (reclassify to expense)

CURRENT LIABILITIES (GL Accounts 2000-2100)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. ACCOUNTS PAYABLE (GL Account 2010)
   Purpose:
   ├─ Track outstanding supplier invoices
   ├─ Manage payment obligations
   
   GL Entries:
   ├─ Credit: When purchase made (not yet paid)
   ├─ Debit: When payment made
   
   Tracking:
   ├─ Model: Purchase (existing) + GL Entry
   ├─ Supplier statement reconciliation
   ├─ Payment aging (due, overdue)
   ├─ Discount tracking (early payment discounts)
   
   Payment Workflow:
   ├─ Purchase Approved → GL Entry (Debit Inventory/Expense, Credit AP)
   ├─ Payment Made → GL Entry (Debit AP, Credit Bank)
   ├─ Reconciliation: AP GL = Outstanding Supplier Invoices

2. SALES TAX PAYABLE (GL Account 2020)
   Purpose:
   ├─ Track sales tax collected from customers
   ├─ Manage tax payment obligations to government
   
   GL Entries:
   ├─ Credit: When sale made (sales tax collected)
   ├─ Debit: When tax paid to government
   
   Current System:
   ├─ Tax calculated on every sale
   ├─ Needs GL posting integration
   
   GL Posting:
   ├─ Sale Entry: Debit Bank, Credit Revenue, Credit Tax Payable
   ├─ Tax Payment: Debit Tax Payable, Credit Bank
   
   Reconciliation:
   ├─ Sum all sales tax from sales
   ├─ Deduct any input tax credits (purchases)
   ├─ Pay balance to tax authority
   ├─ File quarterly/monthly returns

3. INCOME TAX PAYABLE (GL Account 2030)
   Purpose:
   ├─ Track corporate income tax liability
   ├─ Government requirement
   
   GL Entries:
   ├─ Credit: Monthly/quarterly accrual
   ├─ Debit: When tax payment made
   
   Implementation:
   ├─ Phase 4: Add tax calculation service
   ├─ Based on: Net Income = Revenue - All Expenses
   ├─ Apply tax rate (Nigeria: currently 30%)
   
   Example:
   ├─ Net Income (Year): ₦1,000,000
   ├─ Tax Rate: 30%
   ├─ Tax Due: ₦300,000
   ├─ GL Entry: Debit Tax Expense, Credit Tax Payable

4. EMPLOYEE WITHHOLDING PAYABLE (GL Account 2040)
   Purpose:
   ├─ Track PAYE (Pay As You Earn) withheld from employee salaries
   ├─ Manage remittance to tax authority
   
   GL Entries:
   ├─ Credit: When salary paid (PAYE deducted)
   ├─ Debit: When remitted to government
   
   Current System:
   ├─ Integrated with payroll system
   ├─ Needs GL posting


SHORT-TERM LOANS (GL Account 2100)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose:
├─ Track short-term debt (due within 12 months)
├─ Bank overdrafts, lines of credit
├─ Short-term loans from family/investors

GL Entries:
├─ Credit: When loan received
├─ Debit: When loan repaid

Interest Tracking:
├─ GL Account 8010: Interest Expense
├─ Accrued monthly (Debit Expense, Credit Payable)
├─ Paid when loan is repaid


LONG-TERM LIABILITIES (GL Accounts 2200+)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

LONG-TERM LOANS (GL Account 2200)
Purpose:
├─ Track long-term debt (due after 12 months)
├─ Bank loans, mortgages
├─ Long-term financing

GL Entries:
├─ Credit: When loan received
├─ Debit: When principal repaid (annually)
├─ Interest: Monthly accrual (Debit 8010, Credit Interest Payable)

Loan Tracking:
├─ Loan amount, rate, term
├─ Monthly/quarterly payment schedule
├─ Principal vs interest breakdown

Balance Sheet Presentation:
├─ Current portion (due within 12 months) → 2100 or 2300
├─ Long-term portion → 2200

ACCRUED EXPENSES (GL Account 2300)
Purpose:
├─ Track expenses incurred but not yet paid
├─ Utilities, salaries, bonuses
├─ Professional fees accrued

GL Entries:
├─ Credit: When expense is accrued
├─ Debit: When payment made
├─ Monthly: Accrue estimated expense

================================================================================
                    ASSET & LIABILITY POLICIES
================================================================================

CASH MANAGEMENT POLICY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Daily Procedures:
├─ Count cash at end of business day
├─ Record count in system (CashPosition)
├─ Deposit to bank or secure location
├─ Investigate variances > ₦500

Variance Handling:
├─ Small variances (< ₦500): Expense to "Cash Shortage"
├─ Large variances (> ₦500): Investigate before closing
├─ Recurring shortages: Review processes/training

Cash Limits:
├─ Maximum cash on hand: ₦50,000 (or set per business)
├─ Excess cash: Deposit to bank
├─ Petty cash: Keep separate, not for large expenses


BANK RECONCILIATION POLICY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Frequency: Monthly (minimum)

Procedure:
1. Obtain bank statement
2. Match GL balance to bank statement
3. Identify timing differences (in-transit items)
4. Investigate unexplained discrepancies
5. Record reconciling entries if needed
6. Sign off and file

Timeframe:
├─ Complete by 5th of following month
├─ Escalate unresolved items to CFO


INVENTORY POLICY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Physical Count:
├─ Quarterly: Full count of all items
├─ Monthly: Sample count (high-value items)
├─ Investigate variances > 2%

Adjustment:
├─ Variance adjustment entry to GL
├─ Debit: COGS (if shortage)
├─ Credit: Inventory

Damage/Waste:
├─ Log all damaged items immediately
├─ Remove from inventory (GL adjustment)
├─ Track for cost analysis


ACCOUNTS PAYABLE POLICY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Payment Terms:
├─ Default: Net 30 days
├─ Negotiate early payment discounts
├─ Track "2/10 Net 30" opportunities

Payment Authorization:
├─ Under ₦50,000: Manager approval
├─ ₦50,000-₦500,000: Director approval
├─ Over ₦500,000: Board approval

Reconciliation:
├─ Monthly supplier statement matching
├─ Investigate discrepancies within 5 days
├─ Prepare for month-end closing

Key Metrics
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities
├─ Target: 1.5 - 2.0 (enough to cover short-term obligations)

Quick Ratio = (Current Assets - Inventory) / Current Liabilities
├─ Target: 1.0 - 1.5 (liquid assets available)

Debt-to-Equity = Total Liabilities / Total Equity
├─ Target: < 1.0 (more equity than debt)

Debt Service Ratio = EBITDA / Total Debt Payments
├─ Target: > 2.0 (can cover debt payments)
