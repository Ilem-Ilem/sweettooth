CURRENT SYSTEM OVERVIEW
================================================================================

The company currently uses two Excel-based reports:

1. DAILY SALES REPORT FOR DECEMBER 2025
   Purpose: Track daily POS revenue by payment method
   Location: Root directory
   Scope: Daily sales tracking from multiple payment channels

2. DBP 2025 (Daily Bank Positions)
   Purpose: Track daily bank account balances and cash positions
   Location: Root directory
   Scope: Multi-bank reconciliation with balance forward approach

                    DBP 2025 STRUCTURE (Daily Bank Positions)
================================================================================

Purpose: Multi-bank daily balance reconciliation
Scope: Daily opening and closing balances for all active bank accounts
Timeline: Daily updates with running balances

BANK ACCOUNTS TRACKED:
├─ Stanbic IBTC (with interest rate: 0.005375)
├─ GTB (with interest rate: 0.005375)
├─ Zenith (with interest rate: 0.0050001)
├─ Access (no rate listed)
├─ Titan Trust (with interest rate: 0.005375)
├─ Jaiz (with interest rate: 0.005375)
├─ Keystone (no rate listed)
├─ UBA (with interest rate: 0.005375)
├─ Wema (no rate listed)
├─ Stateside (no rate listed)
├─ Sales Cash (physical cash from sales)
├─ Petty Cash (small denomination cash)

DAILY STRUCTURE (Per Bank/Per Day):
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

OPENING BALANCE
├─ Starting balance from previous day
├─ Example: 01/12/2025 - Stanbic IBTC: ₦102,139,113.66

INFLOWS (Money In):
├─ POS - Sales (POS machine deposits)
├─ TRANSFER - Sales (Inter-bank transfers from sales)
├─ REVERSED TRANSFER (Refunds or reversals)
├─ OTHER INCOME (Interest, fees recovered, etc.)
├─ CASH DEPOSIT (Direct cash deposits to bank)

OUTFLOWS (Money Out):
├─ TRANSFER - Expenses (Payments to suppliers/expenses)
├─ CASH WITHDRAWAL (ATM or over-the-counter withdrawals)
├─ CHARGEBACKS (Failed/reversed transactions)
├─ CHARGES (Bank service charges, fees)

UNAVAILABLE BALANCE
├─ Balance after inflows/outflows
├─ Note: Shows uncleared funds

UNREFLECTED ITEMS (Pending/In-Transit):
├─ Unreflected Transfer B/F (Balance Forward - transfers in transit)
├─ Unreflected POS B/F (POS deposits not yet cleared)
├─ POS - Sales unreflected (POS sales not yet shown in bank)
├─ TRANSFER - Sales Unreflected (Transfers not yet cleared)
├─ Unreflected Transfer Received (Transfers received but not cleared)
├─ Unreflected POS Received (POS received but not cleared)
├─ Unreflected Transfer C/D (Carry Down - transfers moving forward)
├─ Unreflected POS C/D (Carry Down - POS moving forward)

AVAILABLE BALANCE
├─ Final usable balance
├─ = Unavailable Balance + Unreflected items
├─ Shows actual available cash

DATABASE STRUCTURE MAPPING
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

NEW TABLE: bank_accounts
└─ Will replace manual Excel bank columns
   Fields:
   - id
   - bank_name (e.g., "Stanbic IBTC", "GTB", "UBA")
   - bank_code (ISO standard or internal code)
   - account_number
   - account_type (current, savings, operating)
   - opening_balance
   - interest_rate (from DBP - e.g., 0.005375)
   - is_active
   - timestamps

NEW TABLE: daily_bank_positions
└─ Will replace Excel DBP 2025 structure
   Fields:
   - id
   - bank_account_id
   - position_date
   - opening_balance
   - inflows (total)
   - outflows (total)
   - unavailable_balance
   - unreflected_transfers_bf
   - unreflected_pos_bf
   - unreflected_transfers_cd
   - unreflected_pos_cd
   - available_balance
   - variance_notes
   - created_at
   - updated_at

NEW TABLE: daily_bank_transactions
└─ Details of each flow (inflows/outflows)
   Fields:
   - id
   - daily_bank_position_id
   - transaction_type (POS, Transfer, Withdrawal, Deposit, Charge, etc.)
   - transaction_subtype (e.g., "Transfer-Sales", "Cash-Deposit")
   - amount
   - description
   - reference_number
   - cleared_date (when it appears in actual bank balance)
   - status (pending, cleared, reversed)
   - created_at

NEW TABLE: cash_positions
└─ Will track physical cash (Sales Cash, Petty Cash)
   Fields:
   - id
   - cash_type (sales_cash, petty_cash)
   - position_date
   - opening_balance
   - receipts (from sales)
   - withdrawals (to bank deposits)
   - closing_balance
   - discrepancies (if any)
   - created_at

MAPPING TO GL ACCOUNTS (Integration with GlAccount):
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Daily Sales Report → GL Entries:
─────────────────────────────────
Sales Amount (ACTUAL SALES) 
├─ GL Account: 4010 (Sales Revenue - Retail)
├─ When Recorded: End of sales day
├─ Entry: Debit Cash/Bank, Credit Sales Revenue
├─ Amount: Total POS + Cash + Transfers

Cash Received
├─ GL Account: 1010/1020 (Cash accounts)
├─ When Recorded: Immediately on receipt
├─ Entry: Debit Cash, Credit Sales Revenue
├─ Amount: Direct hand cash received

POS Deposits
├─ GL Account: 1050/1060 (Bank accounts)
├─ When Recorded: When cleared at bank
├─ Entry: Debit Bank, Credit Cash/Suspense
├─ Amount: POS terminal deposits

Inter-bank Transfers
├─ GL Account: 1050/1060 (Bank accounts)
├─ When Recorded: When received (actual date)
├─ Entry: Debit Bank A, Credit Bank B
├─ Amount: Transfer amount


Daily Bank Positions → GL Entries:
──────────────────────────────────
Opening Balance
├─ GL Account: 1050/1060 (Bank accounts)
├─ Balance Forward: Previous day closing

Inflows
├─ POS Sales: 1050 (Bank) to 4010 (Revenue)
├─ Transfers: 1050/A to 1050/B (Inter-bank)
├─ Other Income: 1050 (Bank) to 4040 (Other Income)
├─ Deposits: 1050 (Bank) to 1010 (Cash)

Outflows
├─ Expense Transfers: 1050 (Bank) to 6000s (Expenses)
├─ Withdrawals: 1010 (Cash) to 1050 (Bank)
├─ Charges: 7030 (Bank Charges) to 1050 (Bank)

Bank Charges
├─ GL Account: 7030 (Bank Charges)
├─ When Recorded: When notified by bank
├─ Entry: Debit Bank Charges, Credit Bank Account
├─ Amount: Service charges, fees, etc.

Unreflected Items
├─ GL Account: 1050 (Bank) - Suspense
├─ When Recorded: When item is recorded vs when it clears
├─ Handling: Reversing entries when cleared
├─ Amount: Pending items shown separately


DAILY WORKFLOW INTEGRATION
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Morning (Start of Day):
├─ Previous day's closing balance becomes today's opening
├─ System queries current GL Bank Account balances
├─ Alerts for any discrepancies
├─ Status: Ready for transactions

During Day (Transactions):
├─ POS sales logged automatically (from system)
├─ Cash received logged (from POS reconciliation)
├─ Manual transfers recorded
├─ Each transaction posts to GL in real-time

End of Day (Reconciliation):
├─ Daily Sales Report generated (from POS data)
├─ Bank position calculated (from GL bank accounts)
├─ Discrepancies identified and flagged
├─ Unreflected items tracked

Next Morning (Bank Update):
├─ Actual bank statement checked
├─ Cleared items updated
├─ Reversals recorded
├─ New opening balances set

VARIANCE & DISCREPANCY TRACKING
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Current Variance Logic (From Excel):
├─ VARIANCE = PORTAL - ACTUAL SALES
├─ If zero: Everything matches (good)
├─ If positive: Portal shows more than recorded sales
├─ If negative: Recorded sales more than portal
├─ Cause: Manual note explaining variance

DB Implementation:
├─ Daily_bank_positions.variance_notes
├─ Daily_bank_transactions.status (pending/cleared/reversed)
├─ Can track timing differences
├─ Can identify missing items

Unreflected Tracking (From Excel):
├─ B/F (Balance Forward): Items from previous days
├─ C/D (Carry Down): Items moving to next day
├─ Allows understanding of cash flow timing
├─ Shows clearing delays from banks

DB Implementation:
├─ daily_bank_transactions.cleared_date
├─ Can show pending vs cleared amounts
├─ Can calculate Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)
├─ Can identify slow-clearing channels
