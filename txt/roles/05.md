# Sales Manager Role Documentation

## Overview
The **Sales Manager** role oversees all sales operations, cash management, and sales team performance.

## Access Level
- **Sales Department Control**: Full control over sales operations
- **Financial Transactions**: Sales and refund processing
- **Team Management**: Sales staff supervision

## Key Responsibilities
- Sales performance monitoring
- Cash register management
- Sales team supervision
- Discount and pricing management
- Sales reporting and analysis

## Permissions
- process-sale, issue-refund, view-daily-sales, close-register
- view-sales-reports, manage-sales-discounts, view-sales-transactions
- edit-sales-transactions, void-sales-transactions, manage-payment-methods
- view-till-records, view-stock-levels, view-employees, view-departments
- manage-staff-schedule, view-analytics, view-dashboard
- view-department-reports, view-hr-reports

## Notes
- Department head level access
- Financial transaction authority
- Sales analytics and reporting
- Team performance management

## Security Considerations
- Financial transaction security
- Cash handling compliance
- Sales data integrity
