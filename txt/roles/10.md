# Chef Role Documentation

## Overview
The **Chef** role manages kitchen operations, recipe execution, and food production quality.

## Access Level
- **Kitchen Operations**: Production execution and control
- **Recipe Management**: Recipe access and modification
- **Quality Control**: Production quality assurance

## Key Responsibilities
- Production order execution
- Recipe following and modification
- Quality control monitoring
- Kitchen staff coordination
- Batch history tracking

## Permissions
- view-production-queue, start-production, complete-production
- view-recipes, manage-recipes, view-batch-history
- view-stock-levels, manage-quality-control

## Notes
- Specialized production role
- Recipe authority
- Quality assurance responsibility
- Production execution focus

## Security Considerations
- Recipe integrity
- Production quality standards
- Food safety compliance
