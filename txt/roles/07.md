# Inventory Manager Role Documentation

## Overview
The **Inventory Manager** role manages all inventory operations, stock control, and supplier relationships.

## Access Level
- **Inventory Control**: Full inventory management capabilities
- **Stock Operations**: Receiving, transfers, and adjustments
- **Supplier Management**: Purchase order and supplier oversight

## Key Responsibilities
- Stock level monitoring and control
- Inventory receiving and transfers
- Purchase order management
- Supplier relationship management
- Inventory reporting and analysis

## Permissions
- view-stock-levels, receive-stock, transfer-stock, adjust-inventory
- create-purchase-order, approve-purchase-order, view-inventory-reports
- manage-suppliers, view-stock-valuation, manage-stock-categories
- view-reorder-levels, manage-reorder-levels, write-off-stock
- view-stock-history, view-production-queue, view-sales-transactions
- view-analytics, view-dashboard, view-department-reports

## Notes
- Department head level access
- Financial impact on inventory value
- Critical for supply chain operations
- Stock accuracy responsibility

## Security Considerations
- Inventory value protection
- Stock movement audit trails
- Supplier information security
