# HR Manager Role Documentation

## Overview
The **HR Manager** role manages all human resources functions including employee management, payroll, and organizational structure.

## Access Level
- **HR Department Control**: Full control over HR operations
- **Employee Management**: Complete employee lifecycle management
- **Organizational Control**: Department and role management

## Key Responsibilities
- Employee hiring and management
- Department structure management
- Payroll administration
- Leave and attendance management
- HR reporting and analytics

## Permissions
- view-employees, create-employees, edit-employees, delete-employees
- view-departments, create-departments, edit-departments, delete-departments
- manage-staff-schedule, manage-leave, approve-leave, view-payroll
- manage-payroll, view-hr-reports, manage-roles-assignments
- view-employee-details, view-analytics, view-dashboard
- view-department-reports, view-branches

## Notes
- Complete HR function control
- Sensitive employee data access
- Role assignment authority
- Payroll and compensation management

## Security Considerations
- Employee data privacy compliance
- Sensitive information handling
- Access control for HR functions
