# Head of Gelato Role Documentation

## Overview
The **Head of Gelato** role leads gelato production operations and quality control.

## Access Level
- **Gelato Production Control**: Full gelato production management
- **Quality Assurance**: Production quality oversight
- **Recipe Authority**: Gelato recipe management

## Key Responsibilities
- Gelato production planning and execution
- Quality control and assurance
- Recipe development and management
- Production staff supervision
- Batch tracking and reporting

## Permissions
- view-production-queue, create-production-order, start-production
- complete-production, approve-production, manage-recipes, view-recipes
- view-production-reports, manage-quality-control, view-batch-history
- view-stock-levels

## Notes
- Specialized gelato production leadership
- Recipe and quality authority
- Production planning responsibilities
- Staff supervision in gelato operations

## Security Considerations
- Product quality standards
- Recipe confidentiality
- Production consistency
