# Head of Production Role Documentation

## Overview
The **Head of Production** role manages all production operations, quality control, and production staff.

## Access Level
- **Production Department Control**: Full control over production operations
- **Quality Management**: Quality control and assurance
- **Staff Supervision**: Production team management

## Key Responsibilities
- Production planning and scheduling
- Quality control oversight
- Recipe management and optimization
- Production staff supervision
- Inventory management for production

## Permissions
- view-production-queue, create-production-order, start-production
- complete-production, approve-production, manage-recipes, view-recipes
- view-production-reports, manage-quality-control, view-batch-history
- edit-production-order, cancel-production, view-production-cost
- view-stock-levels, view-employees, view-departments
- manage-staff-schedule, view-analytics, view-dashboard
- view-department-reports, view-hr-reports

## Notes
- Department head level access
- Can approve and manage production orders
- Quality control responsibilities
- Staff scheduling authority

## Security Considerations
- Production process integrity
- Quality assurance compliance
- Staff management oversight
