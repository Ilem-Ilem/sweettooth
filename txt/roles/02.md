# Managing Director Role Documentation

## Overview
The **Managing Director** role provides full operational control while maintaining executive-level access to business functions.

## Access Level
- **Full Operational Control**: Complete access to all business operations
- **All Permissions**: Automatically granted all available permissions
- **Strategic Oversight**: Executive-level decision support

## Key Responsibilities
- Operational management and oversight
- Business performance monitoring
- Strategic planning support
- Cross-functional coordination
- Executive reporting

## Permissions
**All system permissions are automatically assigned**, including:
- Complete business operations access
- Financial and operational reporting
- User management within scope
- Strategic analytics and dashboards
- System-wide operational control

## Notes
- Similar to MD but focused on operations
- Full access to all business functions
- Actions logged for accountability
- Cannot modify system architecture

## Security Considerations
- Comprehensive access monitoring
- Regular permission audits
- Executive oversight of usage
