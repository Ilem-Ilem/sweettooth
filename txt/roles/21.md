# HR Officer Role Documentation

## Overview
The **HR Officer** role supports HR operations with employee management and administrative functions.

## Access Level
- **HR Support**: Employee data access and management
- **Administrative**: HR administrative functions
- **Limited Authority**: Support-level HR operations

## Key Responsibilities
- Employee information management
- Leave request processing
- Basic HR reporting
- Employee data maintenance

## Permissions
- view-employees, view-departments, view-payroll
- view-hr-reports, manage-leave, view-employee-details

## Notes
- Support-level HR role
- Administrative focus
- Employee data handling
- Limited decision authority

## Security Considerations
- Employee data privacy
- HR information security
- Confidentiality compliance
