# Corner Store Manager Role Documentation

## Overview
The **Corner Store Manager** role manages corner store operations, sales, and inventory.

## Access Level
- **Store Operations**: Full corner store management
- **Sales Management**: Sales processing and refunds
- **Inventory Control**: Basic inventory management

## Key Responsibilities
- Corner store sales management
- Register operations and cash handling
- Basic inventory receiving
- Store operations oversight

## Permissions
- process-sale, issue-refund, view-daily-sales, close-register
- view-sales-reports, view-sales-transactions, manage-payment-methods
- view-till-records, view-stock-levels, receive-stock

## Notes
- Small store management
- Combined sales and inventory
- Independent operations
- Local decision making

## Security Considerations
- Cash handling security
- Inventory accuracy
- Store operations integrity
