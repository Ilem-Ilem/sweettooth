# Corner Store Staff Role Documentation

## Overview
The **Corner Store Staff** role handles day-to-day corner store operations and customer service.

## Access Level
- **Store Operations**: Basic store operations
- **Sales Processing**: Customer sales handling
- **Limited Access**: Essential store functions

## Key Responsibilities
- Customer sales processing
- Daily sales monitoring
- Basic store operations
- Stock level checking

## Permissions
- view-sales-dashboard, process-sale, view-daily-sales, view-stock-levels
