# MD (Managing Director) Role Documentation

## Overview
The **MD** role is designed for executive-level users who need comprehensive access to business operations and strategic decision-making tools.

## Access Level
- **Executive Access**: Full operational control with strategic oversight
- **All Permissions**: Automatically granted all available permissions
- **Cross-Departmental**: Can access all business functions

## Key Responsibilities
- Strategic business oversight
- Financial reporting and analysis
- Operational performance monitoring
- Executive decision support
- Cross-departmental coordination

## Permissions
**All system permissions are automatically assigned**, including:
- Complete financial visibility
- All departmental operations
- User and role management
- Strategic reporting and analytics
- System-wide configuration access

## Notes
- Equivalent to Super Admin in terms of permissions
- Designed for C-level executives
- All actions are logged for compliance
- Cannot access technical system administration

## Security Considerations
- Executive-level access monitoring
- Audit trail for all actions
- Regular access review recommended
