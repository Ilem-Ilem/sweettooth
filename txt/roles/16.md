# Cashier Role Documentation

## Overview
The **Cashier** role manages customer sales transactions and basic sales operations.

## Access Level
- **Sales Transactions**: Customer sales processing
- **Basic Sales Access**: Standard sales operations
- **Limited Financial**: Basic transaction authority

## Key Responsibilities
- Customer sales processing
- Daily sales recording
- Basic sales transaction management
- Stock level monitoring

## Permissions
- process-sale, view-daily-sales, view-sales-transactions
- view-till-records, view-stock-levels

## Notes
- Front-line sales role
- Customer-facing position
- Transaction processing focus
- Basic sales operations

## Security Considerations
- Cash handling procedures
- Transaction accuracy
- Customer data protection
