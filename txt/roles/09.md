# Till Supervisor Role Documentation

## Overview
The **Till Supervisor** role manages cash registers, sales transactions, and front-line sales operations.

## Access Level
- **Sales Operations**: Cash register and transaction management
- **Front-line Supervision**: Sales staff oversight
- **Financial Control**: Daily sales and register operations

## Key Responsibilities
- Cash register management
- Sales transaction oversight
- Daily sales reconciliation
- Payment method management
- Sales staff supervision

## Permissions
- process-sale, issue-refund, view-daily-sales, close-register
- view-sales-transactions, manage-payment-methods, view-till-records
- view-analytics, view-dashboard

## Notes
- Sales operations focus

- Front-line management
- Daily operational control
