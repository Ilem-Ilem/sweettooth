# Supervisor Role Documentation

## Overview
The **Supervisor** role provides team leadership and operational oversight within their department.

## Access Level
- **Team Leadership**: Staff supervision and scheduling
- **Operational Oversight**: Department operations monitoring
- **Limited Management**: Basic management functions

## Key Responsibilities
- Team performance monitoring
- Staff scheduling management
- Operational coordination
- Department reporting
- Quality assurance support

## Permissions
- view-employees, view-departments, manage-staff-schedule
- view-production-queue, start-production, complete-production
- view-recipes, view-stock-levels, view-sales-transactions
- view-analytics, view-dashboard, view-department-reports

## Notes
- Middle management level
- Team coordination focus
- Operational execution authority
- Reporting responsibilities

## Security Considerations
- Team management oversight
- Operational process compliance
- Staff scheduling integrity
