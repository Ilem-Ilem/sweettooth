# Stock Controller Role Documentation

## Overview
The **Stock Controller** role manages inventory control, stock movements, and inventory accuracy.

## Access Level
- **Inventory Operations**: Stock receiving, transfers, adjustments
- **Stock Monitoring**: Inventory level and history tracking
- **Reporting Access**: Inventory reports and analytics

## Key Responsibilities
- Stock receiving and transfers
- Inventory adjustments and corrections
- Stock level monitoring
- Inventory reporting
- Reorder level management

## Permissions
- view-stock-levels, receive-stock, transfer-stock, adjust-inventory
- view-inventory-reports, view-reorder-levels, view-stock-history
- view-stock-valuation

## Notes
- Inventory-focused role
- Stock accuracy responsibility
- Financial impact awareness
- Reporting and analysis

## Security Considerations
- Inventory value protection
- Stock movement integrity
- Audit trail maintenance
