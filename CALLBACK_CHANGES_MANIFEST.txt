productDispatchCallback->completeWithStockUpdate():
- Wrapped in DB::transaction()
- Updates ProductStock (atomic)
- Updates DailyProduce (atomic)
- Updates callback status (atomic)

productionCallback->approve():
- Wrapped in DB::transaction()
- Updates Stock OR DailyProduce (atomic)

- ProductStock during update
- DailyProduce during update
