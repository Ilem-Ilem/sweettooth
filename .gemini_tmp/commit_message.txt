fix(accounting): Initialize Livewire service properties in `boot` method

This commit resolves the "Typed property must not be accessed before initialization" error that was occurring in several accounting report Livewire components. The issue arose because service properties (e.g., `$bsService`, `$isService`, `$tbService`, `$glService`) were being initialized in the `mount` method.

The `mount` method in Livewire is only called on the initial component render, not on subsequent AJAX requests. This led to the service properties being uninitialized when Livewire attempted to re-render the component after an interaction, causing a fatal error.

**Fix**: The initialization logic for the service properties in the following components has been moved from the `mount` method to the `boot` method:
-   `app/Livewire/BranchDashboard/Accounting/Report/BalanceSheetReport.php`
-   `app/Livewire/BranchDashboard/Accounting/Report/IncomeStatementReport.php`
-   `app/Livewire/BranchDashboard/Accounting/Report/TrialBalanceReport.php`
-   `app/Livewire/BranchDashboard/Accounting/Report/GeneralLedgerReport.php`

The `boot` method is executed on every component request, ensuring that the service properties are always initialized before they are accessed, thereby preventing the "must not be accessed before initialization" error and improving the stability of these Livewire components.
