- **Transaction Processing**: 
  - Quick and intuitive sales interface
  - Barcode scanning and manual entry
  - Multiple payment method support (cash, card, mobile, bank transfer)
  - Real-time inventory updates
- **Sales Cart Management**: 
  - Add/edit/remove items from cart
  - Discount and coupon application
  - Tax calculation and application
