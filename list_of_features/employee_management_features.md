# Employee Management Features

## Core Employee Data Management

### Employee Registration & Onboarding
- **Comprehensive Profile Management**: 
  - Personal information (name, contact details, date of birth)
  - Professional information (employee ID, job title, department)
  - Emergency contacts and next of kin information
  - Bank details for payroll processing
- **Document Management**: 
  - Document upload and storage (contracts, certificates, IDs)
  - Document expiration tracking and alerts
  - Secure document access with role-based permissions
- **Automated Onboarding Workflow**: 
  - Step-by-step onboarding process
  - Task assignments for HR and IT departments
  - Welcome email and access credential generation

### Employee Status & Lifecycle
- **Employment Status Tracking**: 
  - Active, inactive, suspended, terminated status management
  - Probationary period tracking
  - Contract renewal management
- **Employment History**: 
  - Complete employment timeline
  - Position and department changes
  - Salary history and promotions
- **Offboarding Process**: 
  - Automated exit workflows
  - Asset return tracking
  - Access revocation and account deactivation

## Organizational Structure Management

### Department Management
- **Department Hierarchy**: 
  - Multi-level department structure
  - Parent-child department relationships
  - Department merging and restructuring
- **Department Configuration**: 
  - Department heads and managers assignment
  - Budget allocation per department
  - Department-specific policies and rules
- **Department Analytics**: 
  - Headcount tracking per department
  - Department performance metrics
  - Cost analysis and reporting

### Position & Role Management
- **Job Position Catalog**: 
  - Standardized job descriptions
  - Position grades and levels
  - Skill requirements and qualifications
- **Reporting Structure**: 
  - Organizational chart visualization
  - Direct and indirect reporting relationships
  - Matrix management support
- **Position Control**: 
  - Open position tracking
  - Approval workflows for new positions
  - Headcount planning and budgeting

## Attendance & Time Management

### Time Tracking System
- **Clock In/Clock Out**: 
  - Web-based time clock functionality
  - GPS location verification for remote clocking
  - Break time tracking and management
- **Leave Management**: 
  - Comprehensive leave types (annual, sick, maternity, paternity, etc.)
  - Leave balance tracking and accrual
  - Leave request and approval workflows
  - Leave calendar and team availability

### Attendance Analytics
- **Attendance Reports**: 
  - Individual attendance summaries
  - Department-wide attendance statistics
  - Late arrival and early departure tracking
- **Absence Management**: 
  - Unplanned absence tracking
  - Absence pattern analysis
  - Automated alerts for excessive absences
- **Time & Attendance Integration**: 
  - Payroll system integration
  - Project time tracking
  - Billable hours calculation


### Employee Appraisal System
- **Review Cycles**: 
  - Annual, semi-annual, quarterly reviews
  - Probationary period evaluations
  - Project-based performance reviews
- **Feedback Mechanisms**: 
  - Continuous feedback tools
  - Peer-to-peer feedback
  - Manager feedback and coaching notes
- **Performance Analytics**: 
  - Performance trend analysis
  - Team performance comparison
  - Performance improvement tracking
